My Top 5 Favorite Animes of all Time

  1. One Piece

    Monkey D. Luffy sets out to find the ultimate treasure, the One Piece, and become the King of the Pirates.

  2. Naruto

    It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a loud, mischievous adolescent ninja who hosts the powerful Nine-Tailed Fox spirit and dreams of becoming the Hokage

  3. Dragon Ball Z

    adventures of Son Goku in his adult life as he and his companions defend the Earth against villains

  4. Attack on Titan

    The story follows Eren Yeager in a world where humanity lives inside giant walled cities to protect themselves from man-eating humanoids known as Titans

  5. Bleach

    The story follows the adventures of the teenage Ichigo Kurosaki after he obtains the powers of a Soul Reaper—a death personification similar to the Grim Reaper
