My Cologne Collection

This is my collection of colognes that i love. Each bottle represents a different scent, memory or personal preference, whether its a fresh everyday fragrance, a bold scent for special occasions or a classic cologne that ive always enjoyed. This collection represents my interest in fragrances and the different scents that have become favorites over time.

Santal 33

Le Labo - Eau de Parfum | Woody & Spicy | Unisex

Santal 33 is a warm, woody fragrance known for its smooth sandalwood scent with hints of cedar, leather, and spice. It has a clean but mysterious character that makes it easy to wear both casually and for special occasions.

Gris Charnel

BDK Parfums - Eau de Parfum | Woody & Spicy | Unisex

Gris Charnel is a smooth and cozy fragrance with a blend of fig, black tea, cardamom and sandalwood. It has a warm, slightly sweet and creamy scent that feels sophisticated without being too overpowering.

Louis Vuitton Imagination

Eau de Parfum | Citrus & Amber | Fresh & Clean

Imagination is a bright and refreshing fragrance with notes of Citrus, black tea, ginger and ambrox. It has a clean, airy and slightly spicy scent that feels modern and easy to wear.

Baccarat Rouge 540

Eau de Parfum | Amber & Floral

Rouge 540 is a sweet, warm and luxurious fragrance with a distinctive blend of saffron, jasmine, amberwood and cedar. Its a great choice for special occasions or whenever you want a fragrance that gets noticed.

Stronger With You Intensely

Emporio Armani - Eau de Parfum | Amber & Vanilla | Warm & Sweet

Stronger With You Intensely is a warm and sweet fragrance with notes of pink pepper, toffee, cinnamon, lavender and vanilla. It has a rich, cozy scent with a smooth sweetness that makes it enjoyable during cold weather and nighttime.

Tom Ford Ombre Leather

Tom Ford - Eau de Parfum | Leather & Woody | Unisex

Ombre Leather is a bold and sophisticated fragrance centered around rich leather. It has a smooth, dark, and slightly smoky character that gives it a confident feel. It works especially well for cooler weather and special occasions.
